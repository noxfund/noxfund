Referral System

Earn 20% commission across 7 levels on every deposit

🎯 Overview

NOX FUND features a powerful 7-level referral system that rewards you for building and growing your network. Every time someone in your downline makes a deposit, you earn instant commissions—up to 7 levels deep.

💰 Total Commission Rate: 20%
Distributed across 7 levels, paid instantly on every deposit in USDT.

🔐 Referral Lock System

⚠️ Important: Once a user makes their first deposit with a referrer, that relationship is permanently locked. This means:
  • The referrer cannot be changed
  • All future deposits from that user count toward the same upline
  • Prevents referral hijacking and manipulation
  • Ensures stable team structures

💰 Withdrawing Referral Earnings

Referral commissions are paid instantly when someone in your downline makes a deposit. However, they accumulate in your referral balance until you withdraw them.

To Withdraw:

  1. Go to the NOX FUND app dashboard
  2. Check your "Referral Balance"
  3. Click "Withdraw Referral Earnings"
  4. Confirm the transaction in your wallet
  5. Receive USDT instantly
💡 Pro Tip: You can either withdraw referral earnings for immediate income, or reinvest them as new deposits to compound your returns! Many successful users treat referral earnings as "bonus capital" for reinvestment.

❓ Common Questions

Do I need to recruit to earn?

No! You earn 1.5% daily on your own deposits regardless of referrals. The referral system is an optional way to earn additional income.

What if I don't have a referrer?

You can still join and earn 1.5% daily without a referrer. However, you won't help anyone earn commissions from your deposits.

Can I change my referrer?

No. Once locked after your first deposit, the referrer relationship is permanent.

How quickly are commissions paid?

Instantly! The moment someone in your downline makes a deposit, your commission is added to your referral balance.

Is there a limit to referral earnings?

No limit! You can earn unlimited commissions from your network.
