Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about NOX FUND

🎯 Basics

What is NOX FUND?
NOX FUND is a trustless passive income protocol on BNB Smart Chain that pays 1.5% daily returns on USDT deposits. It's completely ownerless with no admin keys, meaning the smart contract runs automatically without any human intervention.
How much can I earn?
You earn 1.5% per day on your full deposit amount. For example:
  • 100 USDT deposit = 1.5 USDT daily
  • 1,000 USDT deposit = 15 USDT daily
  • 10,000 USDT deposit = 150 USDT daily
Maximum total ROI per deposit is 547.5% (365 days × 1.5%).
Is there a minimum deposit?
Yes, the minimum deposit is 1 USDT. There is no maximum limit, but each wallet can have up to 200 separate deposits.
What blockchain is this on?
NOX FUND is deployed on BNB Smart Chain (BSC). You'll need BNB for gas fees and USDT (BEP-20) for deposits.

💰 Deposits & Returns

How long is my capital locked?
Your principal is locked for 45 days from the deposit timestamp. After 45 days, you can withdraw your principal. Your daily ROI is claimable anytime, even during the lock period.
Are there any fees?
  • Deposit fee: 5% (paid by the pool, not deducted from your deposit)
  • Withdrawal fee: 0% (completely free)
  • Gas fees: Standard BNB network fees (~$0.10-0.50 per transaction)
Can I make multiple deposits?
Yes! You can make up to 200 separate deposits per wallet. Each deposit has its own 45-day lock timer and earns independently. This is great for the rolling lock strategy.
When do I start earning?
You start earning immediately upon deposit. ROI accumulates every second and can be claimed anytime.

💸 Withdrawals

How do I withdraw my earnings?
Go to the app dashboard and click:
  • "Claim ROI" to withdraw daily earnings
  • "Withdraw Principal" to withdraw unlocked capital (after 45 days)
  • "Withdraw Referrals" to claim referral commissions
All withdrawals are instant—USDT goes directly to your wallet.
Can I withdraw anytime?
  • ROI: Yes, anytime—even during lock period
  • Principal: Only after 45 days from deposit
  • Referral commissions: Yes, anytime
What if there's not enough liquidity?
The protocol requires sufficient USDT in the pool for withdrawals. If liquidity is temporarily low, you may need to wait for new deposits to come in. Principal withdrawals are prioritized, and any unclaimed ROI is preserved for later withdrawal.

🤝 Referral System

How does the referral system work?
NOX FUND has a 7-level referral structure that pays 20% total commission on every deposit:
  • Level 1: 8%
  • Level 2: 4%
  • Level 3: 3%
  • Level 4: 2%
  • Level 5: 1.5%
  • Level 6: 1%
  • Level 7: 0.5%
Commissions are paid instantly when someone in your downline deposits.
Do I need referrals to earn?
No! You earn 1.5% daily on your own deposits regardless of referrals. The referral system is an optional way to earn extra income.
Can I change my referrer?
No. Once you make your first deposit with a referrer, that relationship is permanently locked. This prevents manipulation and ensures stable team structures.
What's the vanity username system?
You can claim a unique username (3-20 characters) for a readable referral link. For example: nox.fund/?ref=alice instead of ?ref=0x123.... Usernames are first-come, first-served and cannot be changed.

🔧 Technical

What wallet do I need?
You need a Web3 wallet that supports BNB Smart Chain:
  • MetaMask (recommended)
  • Trust Wallet
  • Coinbase Wallet
  • WalletConnect-compatible wallets
How do I add BNB Smart Chain to MetaMask?
  1. Open MetaMask
  2. Click network dropdown (top center)
  3. Click "Add Network"
  4. Search "BNB Smart Chain" or add manually:
  • Network Name: BNB Smart Chain
  • RPC URL: https://bsc-dataseed.binance.org/
  • Chain ID: 56
  • Symbol: BNB
  • Explorer: https://bscscan.com
Where do I get USDT?
Buy USDT from:
  • Centralized exchanges: Binance, KuCoin, etc. (withdraw as BEP-20)
  • DEXs: PancakeSwap (swap BNB for USDT)
  • On-ramps: Simplex, MoonPay (buy directly to wallet)
Make sure you're using USDT on BSC (BEP-20), not Ethereum or other chains.
Why is my transaction failing?
Common reasons:
  • Insufficient BNB: You need BNB for gas fees (~$0.50)
  • No USDT approval: First transaction must approve contract to spend USDT
  • Low gas limit: Increase gas limit in wallet settings
  • Wrong network: Ensure you're on BNB Smart Chain (Chain ID: 56)

🔒 Safety & Security

Is NOX FUND safe?
NOX FUND prioritizes security:
  • Audited: Smart contract audited by HazeCrypto
  • Trustless: No owner, no admin keys, cannot be paused
  • Immutable: Code cannot be changed after deployment
  • Open source: Verified on BSCScan for transparency
However, all DeFi carries risk. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
Is this a Ponzi scheme?
NOX FUND is a transparent DeFi protocol where all logic is on-chain and verifiable. Like many yield protocols, returns are funded by the pool which grows with new deposits. Key differences from Ponzi schemes:
  • Completely transparent - all code is public
  • No central operator to run away with funds
  • Users control their own withdrawals
  • Clear disclosure of how it works
Understand the risks and dynamics before participating.
Can the developers rug pull?
No. NOX FUND is completely ownerless. There are:
  • No admin functions
  • No ownership transfer capabilities
  • No pause functions
  • No upgrade mechanism
The developers cannot access funds or modify the contract in any way. Everything is automated by immutable code.
What are the risks?
  • Smart contract risk: Despite audits, bugs could exist
  • Liquidity risk: Withdrawals depend on pool having funds
  • Market risk: USDT could depeg from $1
  • Sustainability risk: Protocol depends on continuous participation
  • Regulatory risk: Laws around DeFi may change
Always do your own research and never invest more than you can afford to lose.
How do I protect myself?
  • Start with a small amount to test
  • Never share your seed phrase or private keys
  • Use a hardware wallet for large amounts
  • Verify the contract address on BSCScan
  • Don't fall for phishing sites—bookmark the real URL
  • Diversify—don't put all your capital in one protocol
