💬 Community Management

Setting Up Your Community Channels

1. Telegram Group Best Practices

  • Welcome Message: Auto-greeting for new members
  • Pinned Message: Key links and rules
  • Group Rules: Clear guidelines on behavior
  • Admin Team: Delegate moderation
  • Bots: Use anti-spam and welcome bots

2. Discord Server Structure

  • #welcome - Introduction and rules
  • #announcements - Official updates only
  • #general-chat - Community discussion
  • #support - Technical help
  • #success-stories - Celebrate wins
  • #strategies - Share tactics
  • #referrals - Promotion allowed here

Engagement Tactics

Daily Activities

  • Morning motivational message
  • Answer support questions within 2 hours
  • Share one educational post
  • Celebrate team member wins
  • Evening engagement post (poll/question)

Weekly Activities

  • Monday: Weekly goals announcement
  • Wednesday: Live Q&A or AMA
  • Friday: Contest or giveaway
  • Sunday: Week recap and top performer recognition

📊 Track Your Growth

Key Metrics to Monitor

  • Follower Growth: Track weekly increases
  • Engagement Rate: Likes, comments, shares per post
  • Click-Through Rate: Link clicks to app
  • Conversion Rate: Visitors to sign-ups
  • Community Size: Telegram/Discord members
  • Active Users: Daily/weekly engagement

⚠️ Dos and Don'ts

✅ DO

  • Be authentic and transparent
  • Show real results with proof
  • Provide value before asking
  • Respond to questions quickly
  • Build genuine relationships
  • Educate your audience
  • Celebrate community wins

❌ DON'T

  • Spam referral links everywhere
  • Make unrealistic promises
  • Fake results or screenshots
  • Ignore negative feedback
  • Over-promote, under-deliver
  • Violate platform rules
  • Pressure people to join
