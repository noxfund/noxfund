Getting Started

Your complete guide to earning 1.5% daily with NOX FUND

⏱️ Time to complete: 15-30 minutes
💰 Minimum investment: 1 USDT
🔧 Technical level: Beginner-friendly

📋 What You'll Need

  • A Web3 wallet (MetaMask recommended)
  • BNB for gas fees (~$1-2 worth)
  • USDT (BEP-20) for your deposit
  • 10-15 minutes of your time

🎯 Step-by-Step Guide

1
Install MetaMask Wallet

MetaMask is a browser extension wallet that lets you interact with blockchain apps.

Installation:

  1. Visit metamask.io
  2. Click "Download" and install for your browser
  3. Create a new wallet or import existing one
  4. IMPORTANT: Write down your 12-word seed phrase and store it safely
  5. Never share your seed phrase with anyone!
⚠️ Security Warning: Your seed phrase is the key to your wallet. Anyone with it can access your funds. Keep it offline in a secure location. NOX FUND will NEVER ask for your seed phrase.
2
Add BNB Smart Chain Network

NOX FUND runs on BNB Smart Chain (BSC). You need to add this network to MetaMask:

Method 1: Automatic (Easiest)

  1. Visit chainlist.org
  2. Search "BNB Smart Chain"
  3. Click "Connect Wallet" and approve

Method 2: Manual

  1. Open MetaMask
  2. Click network dropdown (top center)
  3. Click "Add Network" → "Add network manually"
  4. Enter these details:
    • Network Name: BNB Smart Chain
    • RPC URL: https://bsc-dataseed.binance.org/
    • Chain ID: 56
    • Symbol: BNB
    • Block Explorer: https://bscscan.com
  5. Click "Save"
3
Get BNB & USDT

You need two tokens:

  • BNB: For transaction gas fees (~$1-2 is enough)
  • USDT: For your NOX FUND deposit

Option A: Buy from Centralized Exchange

  1. Sign up at Binance, KuCoin, or similar
  2. Buy BNB and USDT
  3. Withdraw to your MetaMask address
  4. Important: Select "BEP-20" or "BSC" network for withdrawal

Option B: Use DEX (PancakeSwap)

  1. Buy BNB from an exchange and send to MetaMask
  2. Go to pancakeswap.finance
  3. Connect your wallet
  4. Swap some BNB for USDT (keep ~$2 of BNB for gas)
⚠️ Network Warning: Always use BEP-20/BSC network. Using wrong network (like Ethereum) will result in lost funds.
4
Connect to NOX FUND
  1. Visit nox.fund (bookmark this!)
  2. Click "Connect Wallet" button
  3. Select MetaMask from options
  4. Approve the connection in MetaMask popup
  5. Verify you're on BNB Smart Chain (Chain ID: 56)
✅ Connected Successfully: You should see your wallet address in the top right corner.
5
Make Your First Deposit
  1. Enter deposit amount (minimum 1 USDT)
  2. Optional: Enter referrer address or username
  3. Click "Deposit" button
  4. First time: You'll need to approve USDT spending
    • Click "Approve" and confirm in MetaMask
    • Wait for confirmation (~5 seconds)
  5. Click "Deposit" again and confirm transaction
  6. Wait for confirmation (~5 seconds)
  7. Success! You're now earning 1.5% daily
🎉 Congratulations! Your deposit is now earning. You can claim ROI anytime, and your principal unlocks after 45 days.
6
Claim Your Daily Earnings

After 24 hours, you'll have earned 1.5% of your deposit:

To Claim:

  1. Go to NOX FUND dashboard
  2. Check "Available ROI" amount
  3. Click "Claim ROI" button
  4. Confirm transaction in MetaMask
  5. USDT instantly sent to your wallet!

Compounding Strategy:

Instead of withdrawing, you can reinvest your daily earnings as new deposits to compound your returns exponentially.

7
Optional: Claim Your Username
  1. Go to dashboard settings
  2. Click "Claim Username"
  3. Enter your desired username (3-20 characters)
  4. Confirm transaction
  5. Share your clean referral link: nox.fund/?ref=yourusername
